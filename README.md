# E-Kitabo

E-Kitabo is a digital business book for small shops, made for Android. It helps shop owners keep their sales, stock, expenses, customers and debts in one place, instead of a paper notebook.

## How to install

1. Tap the download link above on your Android phone.
2. If your phone warns that the file can harm your device, tap Download anyway.
3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
4. Open E-Kitabo and sign in with Google or with your email, or create your shop account.

## What E-Kitabo does

- Sales, including credit sales (Banja) and part payments
- Stock and products, with low stock alerts and stock counts
- Expenses and shop running costs
- Customers and what each one has bought
- Staff access, with owner approval
- Daily reports, profit and the money you are owed

Built for small shops in Uganda and across Africa.
